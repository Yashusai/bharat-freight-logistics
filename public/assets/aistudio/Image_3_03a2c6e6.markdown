## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Target Audience
The design system embodies the industrial precision, modern infrastructure, and rapid digital transformation of enterprise logistics across India. It serves fleet operators, enterprise supply chain directors, freight forwarders, and dispatch coordinators managing multimodal transit corridors. The brand balances institutional reliability with high-velocity operational technology.

### Emotional Response
The interface must instill immediate operational confidence, regulatory clarity, and punctuality. The emotional tone is:
- **Resilient & Authoritative:** Built on deep navy tones that project decades of industrial stability and institutional scale.
- **High-Velocity & Urgent:** Punctual and sharp, using safety-orange focal points that prioritize exception handling and rapid action without causing sensory fatigue.
- **Pristine & Organized:** Crisp surfaces and structural alignments that make dense telemetry, waybills, and multimodal freight schedules immediately legible.

### Design Movement: Modern Corporate Logistics
The aesthetic fuses **Modern Corporate** structure with **Industrial Precision Engineering**. It uses subtle technical grid cues, clean 1px structural framing, elevated multi-layered cards, and purposeful safety accents derived from transport fleet markings and logistics hubs.

## Colors

### Color Strategy
The system balances deep infrastructure tones with tactical high-visibility accents to maintain strict visual hierarchy across telemetry dashboards and administrative consoles.

- **Primary (`#0F2744` / Deep Navy):** Represents structural authority, command panels, primary navigation, headers, and core brand identifiers.
- **Tertiary (`#0A192F` / Midnight Anchor):** Used for high-contrast app chrome, top-level navigational headers, and critical status surfaces.
- **Secondary Accent (`#FF6B00` & `#EA580C` / Safety Orange):** Reserved strictly for primary action drivers, transit alerts, critical shipment status indicators, and tracking tags.
- **Backgrounds & Surfaces:** Base canvas sits on `#F8FAFC`, with core content containers elevated on `#FFFFFF`.
- **Slate Neutrals:**
  - `#334155` for high-emphasis data strings, invoices, and titles.
  - `#64748B` for operational metadata, secondary labels, and column headers.
  - `#E2E8F0` for hairline borders, table dividers, and structural bounding boxes.
- **Functional Semantics:**
  - *In-Transit / En-Route:* `#0284C7` (Sky Blue)
  - *Delivered / Cleared:* `#059669` (Emerald)
  - *Delayed / Critical Exception:* `#DC2626` (Crimson)
  - *Scheduled / Pending Dispatch:* `#D97706` (Amber)

## Typography

### Hierarchy & Style Logic
Plus Jakarta Sans provides high geometric stability and open apertures, maintaining legibility when reading registration numbers, bill-of-lading codes, and real-time transit telemetry under varying light conditions.

- **Display & Headlines:** Heavy geometric letterforms with tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) create an industrial, authoritative presence for high-level summaries and metrics.
- **Body:** Neutral and balanced at 15px/22px, optimizing dense tables, audit logs, and freight manifest documents.
- **Labels & Badges:** Uppercase or strong semi-bold treatments with positive tracking (`0.02em` to `0.04em`) ensure fleet categories, consignment statuses, and regulatory codes stand out cleanly against card backgrounds.
- **Data & Numeric Displays:** Set with tabular numeric alignments (`tnum`) to keep financial amounts, axle weights, metric tonnage, and GPS coordinates vertically aligned across table rows.
